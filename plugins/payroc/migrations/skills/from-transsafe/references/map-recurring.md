## Cross-cutting facts

Transport, auth, outcome signalling, `Idempotency-Key` and amount rules: see
[map-protocol-auth-and-results.md](map-protocol-auth-and-results.md) (every Payroc POST/PATCH here except
deactivate/reactivate needs an `Idempotency-Key`; amounts become integer minor units; `currency` becomes a
required alphabetic code). Token, `ttid`, `cof_transid` and `profile_id` translation:
[identifier-translation.md](identifier-translation.md) (`FIELD token`, `FIELD cof_transid` and
`FIELD profile_id` rows). File-local facts:

1. **A TranSafe schedule is one self-contained record; Payroc splits template from enrolment.** TranSafe
   stores type, status, flags, `token`, `bdate`, `edate`, `frequency`, `installment_total`, `amount`, run
   counters and custom fields in one record per schedule. Payroc needs a `paymentPlan` (template;
   `paymentPlanId`, `name`, `currency`, `frequency`, `type`, `onUpdate`, `onDelete` required; optional
   `length`, `recurringOrder`) and then a `subscription` (enrolment; `subscriptionId`, `paymentPlanId`,
   `paymentMethod`, `startDate` required) that may override the plan's amount, length and end date.
   Porting means either a throwaway 1:1 plan per schedule, or grouping schedules that share frequency and
   currency under one plan and overriding per subscription. **That is a design choice for you; either
   works, and neither is the default.** Both Payroc ids are **merchant-assigned strings** (1–48
   characters); TranSafe's schedule `id` is server-generated and numeric.
2. **Frequency vocabularies differ; three TranSafe values have no Payroc equivalent** (table below).
   TranSafe accepts 8 values, case-insensitively. Payroc `frequency` is `weekly`, `fortnightly`,
   `monthly`, `quarterly`, `yearly`, on both plan and subscription. The Payroc plan and subscription
   documentation contains no daily, bimonthly, semi-annual or similar value.
3. **Installment `amount` is the TOTAL on TranSafe and the PER-PAYMENT amount on Payroc.** TranSafe charges
   `amount / installment_total` (integer cents) on each run and `amount − total_paid` on the last one, so
   the final payment can differ by a penny when the total does not divide evenly. Payroc
   `recurringOrder.amount` is the amount of **each** payment (the Payroc repeat-payments guide's
   installment example is a $1200 purchase paid as $100 a month for a year). Copying the TranSafe value
   charges N × the total. A total that does not divide evenly cannot be reproduced by a static plan:
   100.00 over 3 is 33.33 + 33.33 + 33.34 on TranSafe, but 3 × 3333 = 9999 on Payroc. To reproduce the
   remainder, patch `/recurringOrder/amount` before the final cycle (`updateSubscription` bars only
   deleting `recurringOrder`; `replace /recurringOrder/amount` is an official example), or use a `manual`
   subscription and collect each payment with `paySubscription`, which takes the amount in its `order`.
   For **recurring** schedules `amount` is per payment on both sides.
4. **A decline stops a TranSafe schedule; nothing retries.** The scheduler sends one `sale` per due
   schedule; any result other than approval sets status `ERROR`, and the scheduler only selects `ACTIVE`
   schedules (or `PENDING` ones stuck for more than 8 hours). The merchant must fix the card (edit the
   stored account, or the schedule's `token`) and send `status=active`. **On reactivation TranSafe charges
   the failed cycle and every missed cycle back-to-back at the next cron run**: a non-active schedule keeps
   its past `next_run` (an edit never increments it), each successful charge advances one interval from the
   previous `next_run`, and the scheduler re-queries until nothing is due, so N missed cycles become N
   charges in one run. This is skipped only when the schedule's SKIPMISSED flag is set (`skipmissed=yes`
   on an edit that sends no `flags`, or `flags=SKIPMISSED` on add or edit; `skipmissed` is ignored on add
   and whenever the same request also sends `flags`, and any `flags` value replaces the whole flag set) or
   the profile carries `RECURRING_SKIPMISSED`. Payroc: the only failure-related text is
   `currentState.status` `suspended`, "For example, if the customer misses payments";
   `reactivateSubscription` "restarts taking payments" and says nothing about missed cycles. The Payroc
   subscription, plan and repeat-payments documentation says nothing about retries, catch-up billing or
   skipped cycles. **Payroc's documentation does not describe retries or catch-up billing; do not port
   catch-up billing as an assumption, and test decline and reactivation in the test environment.**
5. **When TranSafe charges.** `next_run` is the first second of the due day in the **merchant profile's**
   time zone; monthly-family frequencies anchor on the `bdate` day of month and clamp to the last day of
   shorter months. The charge fires when the server cron runs: `recurring_schedule_time`, default `2200|*`,
   "every day at 10pm local time" (server local; the value on your deployment may differ), at most 100
   schedules per pass, looping, and only for merchant profiles whose status is active. Each charge is a
   `sale` with `priority=low`, `recurring=recurring` or `installment` (which sets the RECURRING or
   INSTALLMENT transaction flag), `ordernum` `R<token>-MMDD` (the raw token **without** its check digit,
   and MMDD in **server-local** time), `comments` "Recurring schedule id N", and the customer's email when
   the customer carries the recurring-email-receipt flag. Payroc `startDate`/`endDate` are dates in
   "Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) during the winter, and Irish Standard Time (IST) during the summer";
   no Payroc text states a time of day or a month-end rule. Expect day-boundary and 29th–31st differences;
   verify in the test environment.
6. **Schedule fields TranSafe's documentation lists but the platform never stores or reads.** A TranSafe schedule has no
   `clientref`, `descr`, `cof_transid` or `cof_authamount` (`clientref` and `descr` belong to the stored
   account). Those keys on a schedule add or edit are dropped **[inferred; not exercised against a live
   system; test in the test environment]**. The list call filters only on `id`, `token`, `type`, `status`,
   `customer_id`, `cardholdername` (substring) and `cardtypes`. So the documented `clientref`,
   `expdate_end` and `account_last4` filters, and the published examples `?clientref=55` and `?active=yes`,
   are **ignored**: they return every schedule. The published installment-edit example body
   `{"active": "no"}` uses a key nothing reads (the status key is `status`), so it disables nothing.
7. **Wire key names differ from the documented key names.** `scheduled_token` → `token`,
   `schedule_id_id` → `id`, `schedule_status` and `scheduled_status` → `status`; the published examples
   send `token`. A schedule's `token` must exist in the caller's token group, be type `NORMAL` and be
   enabled. A stored account cannot be deleted while a schedule references it.
8. **Payroc `updateSubscription` cannot change `frequency`, `type`, `currentState` or `paymentPlan`**
   ("Can't perform any PATCH operation"). Payroc's own realistic PATCH example replaces `/frequency`, and
   the paired example response returns `frequency: yearly`, contradicting that list. Treat the normative
   list as binding and confirm in the test environment before relying on either.
9. **There is no schedule export or import.** TranSafe's token exports carry no schedule data, and the
   published Payroc token import mentions no schedule, plan or subscription. What Payroc states about
   TranSafe schedules pulls in two directions, and none of it is in Payroc's published documentation:
   existing recurring and installment scheduled-payment settings are expected to be retained after
   migration, yet installment payment logic is expected to be handled by the integrator or merchant using
   stored tokens on the Payroc gateway. Confirm with your implementation contact whether Payroc carries
   your schedules over; plan to re-create installment logic unless they confirm otherwise. See the DATA
   row.

### Frequency translation

| TranSafe `frequency` | TranSafe interval | Payroc `frequency` | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| `daily` | +1 day | — | LOSS: no Payroc value; run your own scheduler and charge with `payment` + `standingInstructions` |
| `weekly` | +7 days | `weekly` | same interval |
| `biweekly` | +14 days | `fortnightly` | same interval, renamed |
| `monthly` | `bdate` day, +1 month, clamped to month end | `monthly` | interval same; Payroc month-end rule not stated |
| `bimonthly` | +2 months | — | LOSS: no Payroc value |
| `quarterly` | +3 months | `quarterly` | interval same; month-end rule not stated |
| `semiannually` | +6 months | — | LOSS: no Payroc value |
| `annually` | +1 year | `yearly` | same interval, renamed |

### Status translation

| TranSafe `status` | Payroc `currentState.status` |
|---|---|
| `ACTIVE` | `active` |
| `DISABLED` (caller-set, resumable) | `cancelled` ("Merchant deactivated the subscription"; `reactivateSubscription` resumes it) |
| `ERROR` (set after any non-approved scheduled sale) | nearest is `suspended` **[inferred]**; Payroc does not document what makes a subscription `suspended` or after how many failures, so test it in the test environment |
| `DONE` (past `edate`, or installments exhausted) | `completed` |
| `PENDING` | — (no Payroc status). TranSafe's documentation says "enabled but has not processed any payments"; in practice it is the **in-flight lock** while the scheduler's sale is outstanding. A `PENDING` or `DONE` schedule's **status** (and its `edate`) cannot be changed; other fields stay editable |

A caller may only send `active` or `disabled`; `DONE`, `ERROR` and `PENDING` are rejected ("user not allowed
to specify").
